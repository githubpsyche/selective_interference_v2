## Locked Decisions

- The figure package is the scientific spine; prose should explain the account represented by Figures 3 and 4.
- This section is a model overview, not the formal model specification.
- The target genre is PB&R-style theoretical/model-implementation work: theory-led, readable to cognitive psychologists, and supported by simulations without becoming a technical note.
- The organizational model is closer to Oberauer-style benchmark implementation than Sederberg-style fitting, while Sederberg remains useful as precedent for mapping a disputed empirical inference onto retrieved-context operations.
- The section should proceed by causal sequence: item-context architecture, reminder-linked task encoding, retrieval conditions/test cues, deliberate retrieval control, then handoff to model specification.
- Recognition is central to motivation but should appear here as a supplied-item boundary condition, not as the main simulated retrieval paradigm.
- No standalone VIT/test-phase cue simulation section in this draft. Vigilance-intrusion and vigilance-retrieval measures motivate the retrieval setting and discussion, but should not become an unsimulated central claim.
- Deliberate recall can involve several controls, but the maintained film-retrieval goal/source-like support is the broadest protection against reminder-linked task competition.
- Start-of-film reinstatement and monitoring can help, but should not be written as equally protective.
- Source/goal-like support must not look arbitrarily privileged over exogenous film cues; the prose must explain what operation each cue/control performs.
- Task specificity is discussion/future-work unless the current figure package changes.
- Recognition must not be described as context-free or immune. It is less vulnerable here because the candidate item is supplied.

## Section Role

Develop the retrieved-context account at a conceptual-computational level before equations and parameters. The reader should understand how the trauma-film paradigm becomes item-context operations, how reminder-linked task encoding creates competitors, and why retrieval conditions determine whether those competitors matter.

Reader starts thinking: selective interference might require differently vulnerable memory representations.
Reader should leave thinking: selective interference can arise in one retrieved-context system when task material is linked to film-associated context and later retrieval conditions differ in how they cue, constrain, or supply film material.
Must include: item-context associations; reminder reinstatement; competitor encoding; context-guided retrieval; test-phase cues; recognition as supplied-item access; deliberate control mechanisms; transition to formal implementation.
Must distinguish: competitor encoding from film-trace weakening; context-to-item selection from item-to-context reinstatement; pre-task reminders from test-phase film cues; recognition from context-guided recall; deliberate control from magic voluntary immunity.
Figure role: Figure 3 explains encoding and associative structure; Figure 4 explains retrieval operations and deliberate-control mechanisms.
Do not say: CMR can fit anything; recognition is context-free; deliberate recall is generically spared; task items become film memories; the simulations prove how all trauma-film studies work.
Failure signs: the section reads like methods; figures appear decorative; "context" remains an English word rather than a model construct; retrieval control is a loose bucket; recognition appears as an afterthought.

## Deliberate Retrieval Control

Role: introduce deliberate-recall mechanisms as constrained operations.
Reader starts thinking: voluntary recall simply turns control on.
Reader should leave thinking: deliberate recall uses the same stored associations, but control settings differ in whether they reduce reliance on contaminated context.
Must include: start-of-film context reinstatement; maintained film-retrieval goal/source-like support; retrieval monitoring; hierarchy among controls.
Must distinguish: improving film recall overall from protecting against reminder-linked interference; temporal start reinstatement from source/goal-like film support; exogenous film cue from maintained retrieval goal.
Figure role: Figure 4, especially panels B and C.
Empirical/literature obligations: cite CMR control precedents where appropriate; justify start control later if needed.
Do not say: the model simulates subjective intention directly or that all controls are equally protective.
Failure signs: deliberate recall becomes a magic switch; "goal," "monitoring," and "source" remain undefined; source/goal support looks like an arbitrary boost.
