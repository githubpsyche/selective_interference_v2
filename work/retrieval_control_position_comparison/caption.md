**Candidate Figure 10. Retrieval-control operations produce different patterns of recall across encoded positions.**
The film-reminder and stronger-task-association condition was held fixed, and each panel compares one control operation alone (solid line) with the same baseline containing none of the three control operations (dashed line).
**(A)** Start-of-film context reinstatement.
**(B)** Maintained film-category cue.
**(C)** Retrieval monitoring, implemented here by preventing non-film samples from updating context.
Curves show the probability of recalling an item at each encoded position at any point during the search, based on 5,000 simulated trials per setting.
Shaded regions identify the film, break, task and filler phases.
All panels use the same axes and the control settings from the preceding retrieval-control decomposition; weak film cues are supplied before recall begins and at four-attempt intervals in every condition.
